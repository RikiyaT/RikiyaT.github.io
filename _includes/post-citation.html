{% comment %} How to cite a blog post, after its article: a line of text and a BibTeX entry, each with a copy button. The title, authors (first names first), date and BibTeX key are the page's front matter. The Distill template puts its footnotes and references after this, in the same . {% endcomment %} {%- capture bib_authors -%} {%- for author in page.authors -%} {%- assign names = author | split: " " -%} {{ names | last }}, {{ names | pop | join: " " }}{% unless forloop.last %} and {% endunless %} {%- endfor -%} {%- endcapture -%} {%- assign cite_url = site.url | append: page.url -%} {%- assign lb = "{" -%} {%- assign rb = "}" -%}
